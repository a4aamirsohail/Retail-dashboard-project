# Retail-dashboard-project

This project involved creating a comprehensive dashboard to analyze retail revenue and customer behavior for a retail business. The primary problem was the lack of a centralized, interactive tool for stakeholders to quickly understand business performance, identify growth opportunities, and make informed decisions.

The dashboard solves this problem by providing a high-level overview of key performance indicators (KPIs) and detailed breakdowns, including:

Revenue Trends: Monthly revenue fluctuations to identify seasonal patterns and growth trajectories.

Product Performance: Top 10 best-selling products by revenue and quantity sold, highlighting key drivers of sales.

Customer Segmentation: Analysis of new versus repeat customers, including revenue contribution from each segment.

Geographic Insights: Countries served, providing a global context for the business.

Key insights derived from the dashboard include:

Identification of peak revenue months, allowing for targeted marketing and inventory management.

Pinpointing top-performing products, enabling businesses to focus on promoting and stocking these items.

Understanding the revenue contribution of new versus repeat customers, informing customer retention strategies.

Visualization of the geographic distribution of customers.

In summary, this project delivered an interactive dashboard that empowers stakeholders to monitor retail performance, understand customer behavior, and identify opportunities for growth. The dashboard facilitates data-driven decision-making, leading to optimized sales strategies and enhanced customer engagement.
